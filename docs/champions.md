# Champions

_Generated by `tools/generate-roster.py` from `rules/champions.json`. Edit the JSON, then rerun the tool._

Each champion reads as **champion – family – skills – basic attack**. A skill lists what it is (its kinds) and what an athlete uses it for (its intents):

- **Kinds:** Dash, Blink, Projectile, Line, Aoe, Zone, Summon, Heal, Shield, Cc, Buff, Construct.
- **Intents:** Clear, Engage, Escape, Protect, Objective, Poke, Vision.

Custom champions are labelled by hand. Base-game labels are derived from the game's own skill text and the farming catalogue, so treat them as a first pass.

The same data answers questions in code: `roster::can(name, Intent::Escape)`, `roster::skills_for(name, Intent::Clear)` and `roster::champions_with(Kind::Dash)`.

## Family rules

These are the rules that already apply across franchises. They are listed here; the code at each site applies them.

| Rule | From | To | What happens | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sandevistan_vs_infinity | Cyberpunk: Edgerunners | Jujutsu Kaisen | While Sandevistan is on, David's basic attacks ignore Infinity's damage cut, and 3 hits within 3 s shatter it for its usual 15 s. The gravity smash shatters it too, except inside Gojo's own open domain. | `batch2.rs (David)` |
| parry | ULTRAKILL | everyone | V1 parries any committed strike while his parry is ready, including teleport strikes (Minato, Omen, Dio, Steve's boat and hook, Herobrine): the hit is cancelled and returned. | `batch2::try_parry, called from steve.rs, steve_craft.rs, lib.rs and batch2.rs` |
| walls_block_paths | Minecraft | everyone | Steve's constructions are walls: every champion paths around them, blinks and teleports cannot land inside them, and Levi's cables can bite them. | `steve::walls_up, walls.rs, WallAi` |
| smoke_blocks_sight | VALORANT | everyone | Nobody outside Omen's Dark Cover sees in; someone inside sees only 15000 and nothing outside. Every champion's targeting respects it. | `valorant.rs, danger_zones` |
| stopped_time | JoJo's Bizarre Adventure | everyone | ZA WARUDO stops every caught champion, whatever its franchise; only DIO moves. | `batch2.rs (DIO)` |
