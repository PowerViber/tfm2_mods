# Round 149: Rally joins the game

Native 0.10.85, champion pack 0.2.86 (requires native ≥ 0.10.85).

Rally is the masked volleyball fighter from the Rally 3.3 Lab. Her core is `rally_core.rs`. The native bridge (`rally.rs`) and the Database Editor Lab (`editor/rally.wasm`, built from `native/rally_lab`) both run that same core.

## Decisions applied

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Body art | Approved 2026-11-02 ("Approve current bodies": yellow, black/red and white forms, plus the Self Set helper clone). Recorded with source hashes in `docs/rally-art-review/approval.json`. The effect art was already approved in `docs/rally-results/rally-3.3/effect-approval.json`. |
| Impossible Spike damage | Capped at **70% of the target's max HP**. The edge falloff applies to the capped value. The Receive bank is added once, on top. |
| Positions | All lanes plus jungle. |
| Flash | Keeps the global Flash. |

## Impossible Spike cap

`impossible_packet(amount, max_hp, distance, radius) = blast_damage(min(amount, max_hp × 70 / 100), distance, radius)`. The constant `IMPOSSIBLE_MAX_HP_PERCENT = 70` lives in `rules/rally.json` and is generated into `rally_data.rs` and `editor/rally-data.js`.

| Target | Centre | Halfway | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2,000 HP | 1,400 | 875 | 350 |
| plus Receive bank | + up to 582 | + up to 582 | + up to 582 |

At full force, the existing reserves test now expects 3,500 + 582. Without the cap it gave 10,980.

## Pitfall audit

We checked each of Contender's round-148 pitfalls, and the other known engine traps, against Rally's code.

| # | Pitfall | Result | Detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A basic attack the host accepted gets re-checked and dropped | **Fixed** | The basics loop skipped a hit whenever Rally was busy, and dropped it otherwise. An accepted basic now always lands: as a palm hit within palm range or while busy, and as a volley otherwise. Tests: `accepted_basic_while_busy_lands_instead_of_being_dropped`, `accepted_basic_without_the_ball_lands_as_a_palm_at_host_range`, `accepted_basic_on_a_big_camp_lands`. |
| 2 | Volley blocked by the wall cell its own target stands in (32,000-unit cells) | **Fixed** | The ball-flight wall check now ignores cells that contain the origin or the destination. Only a cell strictly between them blocks the volley. Test: `volley_on_a_camp_inside_a_wall_cell_still_lands`. |
| 3 | Ultimate unlock marker name | **Fixed** | `rally.rs` looked for a marker the game never sets. It now uses `crate::ULT_LEARNED` (`gojo_ult_learned`) through `ult_unlocked(slot, marker)`. Test: `ult_tests::ultimate_unlocks_through_the_passive_ult_marker`. |
| 4 | Impossible Spike damage uncapped | **Fixed** | 70% max-HP cap, as above. Tests: `impossible_centre_packet_is_capped_at_seventy_percent_of_max_hp`, plus the updated reserves test. |
| 5 | Animation CC used as a pose freezes the champion | Passed | `air_hidden` is applied only during Air and Impossible, and only while she is alive. Death resets her to Idle, and an interrupt ends Air. Bind is driven by `Effect::Move`, not by a pose CC. |
| 6 | Reach measured centre to centre | Passed | Basic and skill reach are measured edge to edge. |
| 7 | Off-map positions | Passed | Aim points and storm patches are clamped to the map. |
| 8 | Realm `can_interact` | Passed | Every hit path checks that both units are on the same side of any realm. |
| 9 | Flash during her own locks | Passed | The global Flash stays. It is blocked while Rally is locked by her own charge or Air. |
| 10 | Visible roster idle | Passed | The roster's idle frame is a visible body frame. |
| 11 | Mod Power registration | Passed | Rally is registered with the shared Mod Power table. |
| 12 | Per-tick cost | Passed | Rally fixtures are added to `tick_budget_tests` (Kit::Rally). Cost is comparable to Contender and Raze. |
| 13 | Native Receive and support receipts | Live-pending | Fails closed: with no receipt, nothing is banked. Needs a live check. |
| 14 | Serve vision | Live-pending | The Lab confirms that an unrestricted Serve doesn't reveal fogged enemies. Not yet confirmed in game. |
| 15 | Effect bursts | Live-pending | With two Rallys, up to 35 effects in one tick. They go through the shared effect token bucket. Watch for hitches. |
| 16 | High-pose effects near the top edge | Live-pending | The effect clamp near the top map edge still needs a visual check. |
| 17 | Career migration | Live-pending | Existing careers keep their old roster. Rally reaches new careers and exhibition matches. The migration tests pass. |
| 18 | Balance | Live-pending | Needs playtests. |
