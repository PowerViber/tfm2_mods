Kratos Lab

Installed game reference: original layered map and stock sprites; exact exported lanes, camps and towers. All 900 collision cells match the wall artwork. 1 art pixel = 1,000 world units; the 160-pixel map border is preserved. Combat/AI remain a Lab model. Artwork review.
Sources and host limitations

The map document comes from the installed native map exporter; art and stats come from the installed bundle. Asset registration is verified; live renderer/camera parity remains unproven. Stock movement, attacks, shields, physical mitigation, visibility and tower aggro are modelled. Full stock skill AI and item builds are not emulated. Sight models avoidance; native Sight refunds damage through healing, with healing reduction unverified. Native wall crossing uses a glide plus hop. Champion radius is extracted; other unit collision radii are model values. Camps use extracted base HP. This is not actual-game acceptance.
