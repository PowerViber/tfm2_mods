Contender Lab

The Shadow Boxer on a training floor: dummies, a jungle camp and a wave. The skills, echoes, stacks and AI are the game's own Rust core (wasm); movement and enemy hits are a simple browser model, not proof of native behaviour. The real-map Lab needs the extracted game reference (Codex).

Click to aim. WASD walks him (or steers the projected shadow). Space throws basic attacks at the nearest enemy. Press a skill again within 3 s (S1: while projected) to recast it with a shadow charge.
