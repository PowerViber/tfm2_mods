Controls, roles and mode presets

The Lab toolbar now offers Yellow, Black Thunder and White Storm starting forms, plus an armed-Black-Thunder checkbox. Switching a preset restarts the arena with full resources; recordings retain the selection.

Q Receive selects Receiver. E / Shift+E Set selects Setter. Space starts a directional sprint; Space again is an optional jump and selects Spiker. Hold WASD to run independently of mouse aim. The ball stays held until takeoff. R opens Serve's fixed V and stops walking. Shift+R separately arms Black Thunder. F contacts the ball.

Serve applies +70% final contact height once. This capture uses Black's permanent tier, Self Set and three charges: 11.73 m. White Storm blinks to the apex, requires a timed contact and earns its permanent role tier only after landing a hit.

White Storm — actual Lab capture

White body, branching aura, ball trail, fixed 560000-unit firing sector, enlarged 90000-unit Serve crater and visible contact height. The blink grants no invulnerability. Prototype: White replaces Black's role bonuses with +2.5 m / +15pp Setter / +20pp Receiver, clearing on death; reduction remains capped at 95%.

Black Thunder and crater

Prototype 30-second finisher cooldown, independent of Serve's 75 seconds. Radius 65000, one 0.5-second knock-up, then a four-second crater with 30% slowing. No repeated knock-ups.
