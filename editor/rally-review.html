Controls, roles and mode presets

The Lab toolbar now offers Normal Yellow, Yellow Thunder, Black Thunder and White Storm starting forms, plus an armed-Black-Thunder checkbox. Switching a preset restarts the arena with full resources; recordings retain the selection.

Q Receive selects Receiver. E / Shift+E Set selects Setter. Space starts a directional sprint; Space again is an optional jump and selects Spiker. Hold WASD to run independently of mouse aim. The ball stays held until takeoff. R opens Serve's fixed V and stops walking. Shift+R separately arms Black Thunder. F contacts the ball.

Serve applies +70% final contact height once. This capture uses Black's permanent tier, Self Set and three charges: 11.73 m. Contact height alone selects normal (<4 m), yellow (4–<6 m), Black (6–<10 m) or White (10 m+). White blinks only on a successful contact. Forms and their ball speed persist until death even after lower contacts; no later hit is required.

White Storm — actual Lab capture

White body, branching aura, ball trail, fixed 560000-unit firing sector, enlarged 90000-unit Serve crater and visible contact height. The blink grants no invulnerability. Earlier contacts during this same high-capable arc can release yellow or Black attacks. Prototype: White replaces Black's role bonuses with +2.5 m / +15pp Setter / +20pp Receiver, clearing on death; reduction remains capped at 95%.

Ordinary gold Thunder

Prototype spike range 240000; impact radius 45000. Movement, contact and ball travel use the same shared core as native gameplay. Every released spike returns, including misses. Run-up is 2× speed for three seconds with a repeating gauge and optional jump.

Black Thunder and crater

Separate command: three charges and at least 60 Flow; spends both resources. Serve always uses a 90000-radius impact and crater, even before White Storm; regular Black Thunder retains 65000. Prototype 30-second paid jump-boost cooldown, independent of Serve's 75 seconds. Radius 65000, one 0.5-second knock-up, then a four-second crater with 30% slowing. No repeated knock-ups.

All bodies and helper poses

Actual size and 2×, masked and unmasked. Self Set comes from the visual clone. It supplies no extra unit, collision or attacks.
