MASTERY CHAMPION / 02

Vesper,
the Rift Gauntlets.

Punch a hole in the world. Tear it open. Don't let it break you.

The left gauntlet holds 5 pure orbs (overload it to 9 and it may crack); the right holds hexorbs and the Hexcore, which researches from 70% to an Awakening. Any orb opens any of the 100 dimensions as a rift, a rift punch or a rift swipe: 300 different effects. Shatter rips every rift wide (never hurting allies). Everything is available at every rank: mastery only changes how well she plans.

          DISTANCE HAS A COST. SO DOES GREED.

          A punch through a chain keeps 90% − 30% × (length / 120000)² per link, at least 10%. Every source of her damage shares one limit of 35% of a target's max HP per 2 s; stuns, roots, silences, disarms and banishes share 60 ticks per 3 s; forced movement is capped at 2 per 2 s. Stress at 100 cracks the left gauntlet (8 s offline); 100 corruption breaks the right one (12 s). Allies are never hurt by either.
