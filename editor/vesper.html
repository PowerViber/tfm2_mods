          DISTANCE HAS A COST.

          Each link keeps 90% − 30% × (length / 120000)², at least 10%: three short links beat one long jump. Every source of her damage shares one limit of 35% of a target's max HP per 2 s; stuns, roots, silences, disarms and banishes share 60 ticks per 3 s; forced movement is capped at 2 per 2 s. Strain at 100 is a Breach.
