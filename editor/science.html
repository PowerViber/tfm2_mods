PRECISION, THROUGH PRACTICE

Eight mastery ranks

All 75 recipes are always available, and rank never adds raw power. Higher mastery improves charge allocation, review and the choice of long three-science chains, so a Student stumbles into lab accidents and a Unified Mind lands chains and resonance. Base token speed stays constant.

This comparison runs ten deterministic notebook trials per rank through all 75 recipes. It measures charge and typing errors, lab accidents and how many of the 50 recipes land as complete chains; it does not simulate the game’s AI planner or combat engine.

POWER HAS A SHAPE.

Each experiment emits at most (160 + 0.8 × max(AP, AD)) × its chain payoff in raw damage, subject to its allocated outputs; a Unified strike adds 70 + 0.5 × max(AP, AD). Per target: at most 35% maximum HP over two seconds from this kit’s emitted skill damage. Engine item procs and ordinary attacks follow the game’s rules.

Foreign projectiles receive protective interception; native scientific packets can bend, split, reflect and orbit. The preview is schematic. Final collisions, mitigation and item interactions require an in-game match.
